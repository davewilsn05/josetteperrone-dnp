How this helps

Practical, clinically grounded, and built for real teams.

What Josette helps healthcare teams work through

Her sessions turn familiar pressure points into practical language: burnout that is not reduced to personal weakness, communication that protects safety, leadership that supports human beings, and resilience that does not ask clinicians to simply endure more.

Why her clinical background matters

Josette speaks from the full climb of nursing practice: emergency and trauma nursing, nursing education, family nurse practitioner preparation, and Doctor of Nursing Practice work on burnout mitigation. That range helps her connect with staff, students, educators, and leaders.

Strong-fit events
