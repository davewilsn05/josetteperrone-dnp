Speaking with clinical credibility

Built for high-pressure teams that cannot afford burnout.

Josette’s story spans the full climb of nursing practice: BSN-prepared registered nurse, master’s-prepared nurse educator, family nurse practitioner, and Doctor of Nursing Practice. Her work has moved between high-acuity emergency care, trauma response, nursing education, online learning, and professional communication.

Her talks are designed for healthcare audiences and broader professional teams that need renewed confidence, safer working cultures, stronger communication, and a clearer path through pressure without asking people to simply push harder.

About Josette

A speaker with the clinical range to connect across the room.

Josette Perrone, DNP, MSN-Ed, FNP-C, RN has built her career through emergency and trauma nursing, advanced clinical training, nursing education, and communication. She has cared for patients in high-acuity settings, mentored novice nurses and travel staff, taught nursing students, and studied burnout mitigation through doctoral work.

Her teaching philosophy is built around psychological safety, active learning, role modeling, mentorship, and making complex ideas accessible. Her speaking style is warm, direct, practical, and rooted in the real experiences of people doing difficult work under pressure.
